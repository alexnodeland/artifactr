build: stackr provisions the libraries' dashboards from the checkout

In lattice the libraries' Grafana dashboards sit beside stackr, so stackr's compose.yaml mounts
each library's deploy/grafana/dashboards into Grafana as a folder of its own, next to stackr's.
What moved them between repositories retires: the libraries' Release assets workflows and
artifactr's test of their packaging step, and stackr's scripts/fetch-dashboards, its make
target and its pins in versions.env. The libraries still generate their dashboards and test them
against their metric registries.

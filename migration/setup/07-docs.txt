docs: one site for the family, with a section per package

Each package's pages move to docs/<package>/ with git mv, so their history follows them, and
one mkdocs.yml builds the site, for lattice.alexnodeland.com. Each section keeps its order, its
old nav, in docs/<package>/.nav.yml. relayr's, which had no site yet, lists its decisions, its
plan and its brand; its accepted ADRs link to an index of its RFCs and to a brand page that its
foundation hadn't written, which are added so the strict build holds.

- One build resolves the references between packages, so the evalr inventory goes, and
  mkdocstrings finds every package in the workspace's environment, without paths.
- The Sphinx-roles extension, a copy in artifactr and one in reflexr, is one script at the root
  that covers both, and the list check, a copy in every package, is another.
- Pages include files by their path from the root: each package's CHANGELOG.md and schemas, and
  the files stackr's reference pages describe.
- One CONTRIBUTING.md, code of conduct and security policy, at the root, take in what was each
  package's: its commands, and its scope notes. Their pages are the family's, and each package
  keeps its changelog page. Each package keeps its LICENSE, which its wheel ships, and the root
  has the same one.
- The site's palette is key, the family's neutral ink, until lattice's brand ADR, and each
  section's home opens with its package's lockup as before.
- lattice's README, which also says what grantr and portalr will be.
- The build's tools are the root's docs group. lattice:docs builds the site strictly, and
  stackr:reference checks stackr's reference pages in their new place.
